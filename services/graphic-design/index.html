Graphic design

Graphic design & branding

Considered visual design for businesses that need to look consistent, recognisable and ready to communicate across print and digital channels.

A coherent visual system

More than an isolated logo or one-off post.

Graphic design works best when every piece feels connected. Justy Media can develop a new visual direction or extend the identity you already have into useful, consistent materials.

The scope can be a focused piece of artwork or ongoing design support, with files prepared for the channel and people who will actually use them.

Capabilities

Design support across identity, print and screen.

Brand identities

Core visual direction, colour, typography and practical guidance for consistent use.

Logo development

New marks and considered refinements, supplied in useful formats for print and digital work.

Print artwork

Brochures, leaflets, marketing materials and event graphics prepared for production.

Social media artwork

Reusable post, story and campaign assets that stay recognisably on brand.

Website graphics

Image treatments, diagrams, campaign graphics and supporting visual content for web pages.

Ongoing design support

A reliable route for new materials without reinventing the visual approach each time.

Process

Start with what the design needs to communicate.

  1. BriefDefine the audience, message, format, practical requirements and existing brand context.
  2. DirectionEstablish the strongest visual route and agree it before expanding the work.
  3. RefinementDevelop the selected direction with focused feedback and careful production detail.
  4. DeliverySupply correctly prepared files and a clear explanation of what each one is for.
