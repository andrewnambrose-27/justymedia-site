Web design

Web design & development in the Peak District

A website should make your offer clear and give customers an easy next step. I design and build responsive websites for businesses in Buxton, Leek, the Peak District and across the UK.

Who it is for

For smaller businesses that need a more useful website.

This service suits new businesses building a credible first presence, established organisations whose site no longer reflects them, and teams that need a focused campaign page or practical online tool.

The aim is not to add technology for its own sake. It is to make the message clearer, the experience easier and the route to enquiry more direct.

What can be created

From focused pages to complete small-business sites.

Small-business websites

Structured, responsive sites with clear services, useful content and an enquiry route.

Landing pages & redesigns

Campaign pages and careful improvements to websites that have outgrown their current design.

Custom tools

Lightweight web applications and practical internal or customer-facing utilities where a standard page is not enough.

Analytics & conversion tracking

Measurement planned around meaningful actions, with setup that helps you understand what the site is doing.

Responsive development

Layouts built to work across phones, tablets and larger screens, with accessibility and performance considered.

Updates & maintenance

Ongoing content changes, technical upkeep and measured improvements after launch.

Content management or e-commerce requirements are discussed during scoping so the platform and responsibilities are clear before work begins.

Process

Design and development in one practical workflow.

  1. Discovery and contentClarify the audience, goals, pages, content and any systems the website must connect with.
  2. Structure and designShape the journey and visual direction before detailed development.
  3. Build and testDevelop responsive pages, add tracking where required and test core journeys across devices.
  4. Launch and handoverComplete final checks, publish carefully and provide the information needed to use the finished site.

Ongoing support

A website should not stop at launch.

Support can continue with content updates, landing pages, technical housekeeping, analytics reviews and small improvements. The scope can be agreed around genuine needs rather than a fixed retainer.
