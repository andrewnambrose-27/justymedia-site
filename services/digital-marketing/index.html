Practical digital support

Digital marketing & SEO in the Peak District

Help customers understand your services and find a clear route to enquiry. I offer practical SEO and digital marketing support from the Peak District, serving Buxton, Leek and businesses across the UK.

Who it is for

Give your existing website a clearer purpose.

This support suits small businesses whose service pages are unclear, whose website has technical search issues, or who need to understand which online activity leads to enquiries. It can also help connect a campaign with a useful landing page and consistent creative assets.

I start with your website, content and existing measurement, then explain the priorities in plain language. Depending on the agreed scope, the work can include a technical review, updated page copy and metadata, tracking setup or a practical report with next steps.

Capabilities

Practical ways to strengthen your online presence.

On-page SEO

Titles, descriptions, headings, internal links and page content improved around real search intent.

Technical SEO reviews

Crawlability, canonical URLs, redirects, structured data, sitemaps and performance risks checked methodically.

Search Console & analytics

Setup and configuration that helps you see meaningful website activity and spot problems.

Conversion tracking

Measurement for enquiries, downloads and useful actions—not just a page-view total.

Content improvements

Clearer service pages, calls to action and supporting content shaped around audience questions.

Campaign creative

Email, social and campaign assets that connect the message back to a coherent website journey.

Reporting & support

Regular, understandable review with priorities for the next useful improvements.

Approach

Review, prioritise, improve, learn.

  1. ReviewUnderstand the current site, tracking, content and active marketing work.
  2. PrioritiseSeparate essential fixes from worthwhile experiments and lower-value busywork.
  3. ImplementMake agreed technical, content and creative improvements carefully.
