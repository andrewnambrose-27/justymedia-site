About Us

Here at Justy Media, we have always been interested in capturing memories in photo form for years. Starting out on a 5 megapixel point and shoot camera handed down for use on a vacation, we have grown into a full semi-professional DSLR setup with interchangeable lenses. The love for taking photos has never died, only increased over time, until the point where Justy Media was created in 2020 to offer that passion to everyone looking for photography services.
