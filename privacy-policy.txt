# Privacy Policy
Last updated: 7 August 2026

Justy Media respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information when you visit this website or contact us about photography, design or other creative services.

# Interpretation and Definitions
## Personal Data
Personal Data means information that can identify you, such as your name, email address, telephone number, social-media account or information you provide when making an enquiry or booking.

## Usage Data
Usage Data is collected automatically when you use the website. It may include your device and browser type, pages visited, the time and date of your visit, IP address and similar diagnostic information.

# Collecting and Using Your Personal Data
## Information you provide
We may collect Personal Data when you email us, submit an enquiry, request a quotation, book a service or otherwise communicate with us.

## Cookies and analytics
This website may use essential cookies and privacy-friendly analytics to keep the site working, understand visits and improve the experience. You can manage or remove cookies through your browser settings.

# How We Use Your Information
We use personal information to respond to enquiries, provide and manage services, send information you ask for, improve the website, keep records, protect our rights and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

# Sharing Your Information
We may share information with trusted service providers where necessary to operate the website or provide a requested service. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the rights, safety or property of Justy Media, our clients or others.

# Retention of Your Personal Data
We keep Personal Data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, accounting and reporting requirements. When it is no longer needed, we will delete or securely anonymise it.

# Security of Your Personal Data
We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

# Your Rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of your Personal Data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, please contact us using the email below.
